### Cloud and platform engineering

I have worked with AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, building and operating Kubernetes platforms with tools such as Terraform, Crossplane, and Argo CD. I am especially interested in reusable infrastructure, GitOps, and self-service platforms that make common engineering tasks easier and safer.

### Software and APIs

I build services and integrations with TypeScript, GraphQL, Python, FastAPI, and Go. My experience ranges from designing federated APIs and internal metrics services to creating automation that connects engineering tools and workflows.

### Delivery and reliability

I work on CI/CD, automated verification, observability, and production operations. Tools I have used include GitHub Actions, Azure DevOps, Jenkins, Prometheus, Grafana, Jaeger, and Datadog. I value clear operational signals, safe deployments, and learning from incidents.

### Security and automation

I have worked on vulnerability scanning, secrets management, service-to-service encryption, and identity integrations. I am also interested in AI-assisted engineering when it can reduce repetitive work and make useful capabilities accessible through well-defined, reviewable workflows.

Across these areas, I look for technology choices that reduce toil, improve the developer experience, and produce measurable improvements in reliability, security, or delivery speed.
